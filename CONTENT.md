# Content & growth notes (creator only)

Player-facing UI should stay about playing. Put posting / monetization advice here — not in the games.

## Loop

1. Play **Almost There** until a near-miss or fake platform.
2. Film your face + the fail (5–15 seconds).
3. Post to TikTok / Reels / Shorts.
4. Caption with the site: `games.matthiasamire.com` (or the live game URL).
5. Reply to comments with the same link.

## Why it works

Viewers don’t just watch you lose — they open the link to prove they wouldn’t. That’s the visit.

## Caption starters

- Totally fair btw → games.matthiasamire.com
- Congrats to me for failing again → games.matthiasamire.com/almost-there
- New route every death. Still cooked.

## Clip tips

- Sound on your mic; game SFX help the joke.
- Cut in on the last jump, not the whole run.
- End on the ironic “CONGRATULATIONS” banner.
